# Davey Super-Store-Sales-and-Customers-Behavior
Sales and Activity Data Conducted on Davey Super Store

---

### Project Introduction
An Exploratory analysis carried out on Davey Super store, The Dataset contains 5000 e- commerce transaction recorded Between January 2023 to March 2024, The Data provides insight on Total Customers, Revenue, Purchase Frequency, Payment Methods, Revenue Category, Monthly Sales Trend, Revenue By City and Average Rating.
This Project uses Excel for Data cleaning and Standardization, PowerBI for analytics and Visualization.
This Analysis will uncover insight on customer age to target more, locations where sales management and campaigns need attentions and improvements period of months with good or poor sales performance.

---

### Executive Summary
This Project "Davey Super Store and Customer Behavior" this Analysis focus on customer insight on purchase frequency by Age, Revenue by City, Revenue by Product Category, Monthly sales trends, the data includes OrderID, CustomerID, Date, Age, Gender, City, Product Category, Unit Price, Quantity, Discount Amount, Total Amount, Age Bracket, Payment Method etc.
From insights into the Analysis we discovered that the Business engaged a total of 5000 customers, making a Revenue of $4.31Million and a total sales of 9,594 with an average rating of 3.92%. Age between the Bracket of 35-44 made the highest purchase at 52.2k sales while the age from 64 and above made the lowest purchase at 0.1k sales.
